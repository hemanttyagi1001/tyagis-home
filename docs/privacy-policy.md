# Privacy Policy

**App:** Tyagi's Home (`com.tyagishome.app`)
**Developer:** Hemant Kumar Tyagi
**Contact:** hemanttyagi1001@gmail.com
**Last updated:** 4 September 2026

## Summary

Tyagi's Home does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything
you enter stays on your device. There is no account, no server, and no analytics.

## What the app stores

The app keeps the following on your device only, in a local database:

- Milk records — daily quantities in litres and the prices you configure
- Employee records — names, salary type and amount, allowed monthly leave
- Attendance records — the status you mark for each day

This information is written to your device's private app storage. It is never
uploaded anywhere, because the app has no backend to upload it to.

## What the app does not do

- No user accounts, sign-in, or registration
- No advertising and no ad networks
- No analytics, crash-tracking services, or usage measurement
- No location, contacts, camera, or microphone access
- No selling or sharing of data with third parties, because no data leaves the device

## Sharing a record

The app can export a month's milk or attendance calendar as an image, using your
device's standard share sheet. This only happens when you tap Share and choose a
destination yourself. You control where that image goes; the app does not send it
anywhere on its own.

## Error reports

If the app closes unexpectedly, it saves the technical details of the error on
your device and, on the next launch, asks whether you want to send them to the
developer. If you agree, your email app opens with the details already filled in
and you choose whether to send it.

Those reports contain the error message and stack trace, the app version, your
device model, and your Android version. They do not contain your milk,
attendance, or employee records. Nothing is sent unless you tap send.

## Permissions

Android may list permissions that come from the app's underlying framework
rather than from features the app uses:

- **Internet** — declared by the development framework. The app makes no network
  requests of its own.
- **Run at startup / prevent sleeping** — used to add each day's default entries
  automatically in the background.
- **Storage** — used when you export a calendar image to share it.

## Children

The app is a household record-keeping tool and is not directed at children. It
collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

All app data lives on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage
from Android Settings, permanently removes everything. Since nothing is stored
on a server, there is no separate deletion request to make. If you would like
confirmation of this, contact the address below.
