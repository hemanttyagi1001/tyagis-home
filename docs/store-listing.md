## Full description (4000 max)

```
Tyagi's Home is a simple record keeper for two things every household deals
with: the milk that arrives each morning, and the people who work for you.

Everything is stored on your phone. There is no account to create, no sign-in,
and no internet connection required.


MILK TRACKER

Record buffalo and cow milk separately, in litres, on a month calendar. Set
your rate once and the app applies it to new entries automatically, so a normal
day needs no input at all.

At the end of the month you get the totals that matter: litres taken and the
amount owed, split by milk type. No more reconciling a paper diary against the
milkman's book.


STAFF ATTENDANCE

Add the people who work for you, whether they are paid daily or monthly, and
set how many leaves they are allowed each month.

Mark each day as full day, first half, second half, or leave. The app keeps a
running summary of working days and flags when someone has gone past their
allowed leave, so the month-end calculation is already done for you.


AUTOMATIC DAILY ENTRIES

The app fills in each new day for you using the defaults you set: your usual
milk quantity, and staff marked present. You only open it when something is
different from the normal routine.


SHARE A MONTH

Export any month's milk record or attendance calendar as an image and send it
over WhatsApp or anywhere else. Useful when settling accounts.


PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your records never leave your phone. The app has no server, collects nothing,
shows no ads, and contains no tracking of any kind. Uninstalling removes
everything.
```

## Data safety form answers

The app collects and transmits nothing, so the form is short:

- **Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?** No
- **Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?** N/A
- **Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?** No
  data is collected; uninstalling removes all local data.
